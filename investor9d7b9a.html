Why it gets adopted

Depth wins the buyer. Adoption wins the floor.

Where staff already are
The progress strip sits at the top of the PointClickCare Point of Care (POC) assignment screen — the screen staff already work from. No new app, no new login, near-real-time (~10-min read).
Right work, right license
Observe · assess · code. Each user sees only their role's view — a CNA is asked for an observation, a nurse for an assessment, the MDS coordinator for coding. Never up-scope.
A help, not just a flag
It names the exact plain-language elements missing from the record — accessible across language and background — so a gap is resolved in seconds, not deciphered.
Observe-only by design
No "complete" button, no personnel scoring — ever. A documentation-integrity layer, not surveillance. That posture is both the liability moat and why floors accept it.
Built to be defensible in a survey — and welcomed at the bedside.
The moat

The moat is depth, not novelty.

The barrier is not engineering — it is codified post-acute regulatory judgment: which documentation elements are required to support a given classification, and where a complete record still fails. Sealed inside an engine that never exposes it, that depth is what an integration partner or a general-purpose AI cannot shortcut.
Not a platform integration
Configuring PointClickCare doesn't encode the regulatory standard.
Not a general-AI feature
Broad healthcare AI doesn't carry post-acute classification depth.
Not scrapeable
The selection and crosswalk — built on public CMS material — are the defensible layer.
The asset

Three pillars — one clean object at exit.

1 · The Sealed Engine
The evaluation methodology plus 30 years of codified post-acute domain judgment — held as trade secrets, never disclosed. The core asset, transferable intact.
2 · The Value Set Directory
The compiled crosswalk of CMS documentation requirements to classification — a compilation-copyright asset, separately ownable and licensable, built on public CMS material. Extended with a plain-language, role-scoped documentation-element layer that turns each requirement into the exact missing elements.
3 · RCRF + Automated Reporting
The Regulatory Cross-Reference Function — proprietary consistency-check logic — plus the automated exposure-reporting layer that delivers findings. Trade secret.
+ Name & marks + clean assignments
The category's market identity (trademark to file), plus vendor-IP assigned to Stellar under counsel-reviewed MSAs.
